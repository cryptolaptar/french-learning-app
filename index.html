Mot à Mot

A French vocabulary companion, A1 through C2

0%

Covers the 9 tenses/moods used in everyday spoken and written French. Literary-only tenses (passé simple, subjonctif imparfait, etc.) aren't included. Pronunciations in the table are a simplified, approximate guide — read them like English, stress the last syllable, and "(n)" means a light nasal touch rather than a hard N.

Add this to your iPhone

This is a web app — once it's saved to your Home Screen it opens full-screen, just like a regular app, and stays on your Home Screen. Open it once with internet and it then works offline too. Your progress is saved on your device.

  1. Open this file's URL in Safari on your iPhone (not Chrome — the "Add to Home Screen" option needs Safari).
  2. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up), in the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right. An icon called "Mot à Mot" will appear on your Home Screen.
